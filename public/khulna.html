Khulna

Khulna — The Gateway to the Sundarbans and the Industrial Hub

Khulna is a major divisional city located in the southwestern part of Bangladesh, sitting along the banks of the Rupsha and Bhairab rivers. It is most famous for being the primary gateway to the Sundarbans, the largest mangrove forest in the world and the home of the majestic Royal Bengal Tiger. The city serves as a vital link between the untamed wilderness of the south and the country's booming industrial sector. Historically known as a center for the jute and shrimp industries, Khulna offers a unique mix of urban energy and natural serenity. From the architectural marvel of the Sixty Dome Mosque in nearby Bagerhat to the tranquil river views at the Rupsha Bridge, the region is rich in heritage and biodiversity. Khulna’s quiet, laid-back atmosphere compared to other major cities makes it a peaceful yet essential destination for those looking to explore the ecological wonders of the delta.
